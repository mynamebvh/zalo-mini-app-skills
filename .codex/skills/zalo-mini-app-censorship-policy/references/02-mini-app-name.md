# 2. Mini App Name

> Source: https://miniapp.zaloplatforms.com/documents/zalo-mini-app-censorship-policy/#2-tên-mini-app
> Accessed: 2026-11-02

- Name appropriate to the Mini App's feature/functionality
- Rules for naming the Mini App

## 2.1 Name appropriate to the Mini App's feature/functionality

**Violation**

![Violation](https://miniapp.zaloplatforms.com/documents/img/censorship-policy/v2/2.1.a.png)

- The Mini App name misrepresents the Mini App's functionality

**Compliant**

![Compliant](https://miniapp.zaloplatforms.com/documents/img/censorship-policy/v2/2.1.b.png)

- The Mini App name accurately reflects the Mini App's functionality

## 2.2 Rules for naming the Mini App

**The Mini App name must not be written entirely in uppercase, use generic nouns, contain the words "App", "Mini App", "Zalo", & special characters (#,$,@,!), emoji, or symbols**

**Violation**

![Violation](https://miniapp.zaloplatforms.com/documents/img/censorship-policy/v2/2.2.a.png)

- The Mini App name is written entirely in uppercase, uses generic keywords or nouns

**Compliant**

![Compliant](https://miniapp.zaloplatforms.com/documents/img/censorship-policy/v2/2.2.b.png)

- The Mini App name needs a prefix or suffix to help users easily distinguish the app and clearly identify the owning entity

If this name has registered trademark rights with a state agency, please provide the relevant documents to continue the review process.
