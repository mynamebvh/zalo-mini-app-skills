# 1. Mini App Logo

> Source: https://miniapp.zaloplatforms.com/documents/zalo-mini-app-censorship-policy/#1-logo-mini-app
> Accessed: 2026-11-02

- Genuine logo, not impersonated
- Logo appropriate to the Mini App's functionality

## 1.1 Genuine logo, not impersonated

**Violation**

![Violation](https://miniapp.zaloplatforms.com/documents/img/censorship-policy/v2/1.1.a.png)

- The Mini App uses the logo of another app or another brand
- The Mini App has no logo

**Compliant**

![Compliant](https://miniapp.zaloplatforms.com/documents/img/censorship-policy/v2/1.1.b.png)

- The Mini App uses its own logo

## 1.2 Logo appropriate to the Mini App's functionality

**Violation**

![Violation](https://miniapp.zaloplatforms.com/documents/img/censorship-policy/v2/1.2.a.png)

- The Mini App uses a logo unrelated to the app's functionality or purpose
- The Mini App uses a logo containing a phone number or QR code
- The Mini App uses a logo with a transparent background

**Compliant**

![Compliant](https://miniapp.zaloplatforms.com/documents/img/censorship-policy/v2/1.2.b.png)

- The Mini App uses a clear logo appropriate to the app's functionality and purpose
