# 7. Privacy and Security

> Source: https://miniapp.zaloplatforms.com/documents/zalo-mini-app-censorship-policy/#7-quyền-riêng-tư-và-bảo-mật
> Accessed: 2026-11-02

**Violation**

- The Mini App collects users' personal data without explicit consent
- The Mini App contains malware, or leads through links containing malware
- The Mini App shares users' personal data with third parties without notifying or without the user's permission

**Compliant**

- The Mini App only collects users' personal data with explicit and transparent consent
- The Mini App does not contain unsafe links
- The Mini App does not share users' personal data with third parties unless there is explicit user consent and the third parties are ensured to comply with equivalent security standards
