## 9.1 Review time

- The Mini App will be reviewed within 3 business days, excluding Saturdays, Sundays, and public holidays.

## 9.2 Maintaining Mini App operation

To ensure the platform's operational quality and provide the best experience for Users, your Mini App must meet the following evaluation criteria:

- The Mini App must be verified through a Zalo Official Account (OA), or through valid documents/licenses as required depending on the type of ownership and business line.
- The Mini App's main features are used stably according to the released version.
- The Mini App must maintain a minimum usage level of 10 visits/month.

If the Mini App does not meet one or some criteria within 3 months, the Developer must proactively check and implement timely remediation measures to avoid the Mini App being restricted from display or deactivated on the platform.

## 9.3 Review of Mini Apps in special sectors and conditional business lines

- Mini Apps in certain special fields (Pharmaceuticals, Cosmetics, Functional Foods, etc.) and conditional business lines will be reviewed once Mini App verification and relevant documents are fully completed. See the detailed instructions [here](https://mini.zalo.me/blog/huong-dan-xac-thuc-zalo-mini-app-doi-voi-cac-nhom-nganh-dac-thu/).

## 9.4 Time-limited Mini App deployment

- For Mini Apps deploying a time-limited campaign or event, please clearly state the program's start & end times when submitting the version review request (in the version description/update description field).
- Or display the time information directly on the Mini App's interface (UI).

## 9.5 Support contacts

For any questions or support requests, please contact:

- Mini Apps under the Government Agency/Public Service Unit category: the ["Zalo Digital Transformation"](https://zalo.me/chuyendoiso) OA channel or [minicqnn@zalo.me](mailto:minicqnn@zalo.me)
- Mini Apps in the finance, credit, banking, and insurance sectors: [support@fiza.ai](mailto:support@fiza.ai)
- Mini Apps under other categories: [hotrodoitac@zaloplatforms.com](mailto:hotrodoitac@zaloplatforms.com)
