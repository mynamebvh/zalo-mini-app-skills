# Mini App Censorship Policy — Overview

> Source: https://miniapp.zaloplatforms.com/documents/zalo-mini-app-censorship-policy/
> Accessed: 2026-11-02

Zalo Mini App applies a censorship policy to ensure quality and safety for users. All Mini Apps must comply with content, security, and user experience standards before being approved and released on the platform. This policy helps prevent inappropriate apps and protects the interests of both users and businesses.
