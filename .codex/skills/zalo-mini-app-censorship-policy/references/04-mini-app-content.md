# 4. Mini App Content

> Source: https://miniapp.zaloplatforms.com/documents/zalo-mini-app-censorship-policy/#4-nội-dung-mini-app
> Accessed: 2026-11-02

- Navigation to third-party page links
- Content complies with the law
- Advertising and monetization content
- Virtual items
- Mini App with cash withdrawal/reward payout features
- Mini App with social network features or features that directly compete with products in the Zalo system
- Images and text of the app
- Mini App policy - terms content

## 4.1 Navigation to third-party page links

**Violation**

![Violation](https://miniapp.zaloplatforms.com/documents/img/censorship-policy/v2/4.1.a.png)

- Encouraging users to download a separate app
- Directing users to log in with Google, Facebook, etc.

**Compliant**

![Compliant](https://miniapp.zaloplatforms.com/documents/img/censorship-policy/v2/4.1.b.png)

- No navigation to links outside the Mini App

Do not navigate users outside the app, except to display documents such as the Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, etc. In this case, the Developer must hide or disable the link/button leading outside, or embed the document directly in the Mini App as a popup.

## 4.2 Content complies with the law

**Violation**

![Violation](https://miniapp.zaloplatforms.com/documents/img/censorship-policy/v2/4.2.a.png)

- Content that is misleading, fraudulent, or fake and prohibited by law

**Compliant**

![Compliant](https://miniapp.zaloplatforms.com/documents/img/censorship-policy/v2/4.2.b.png)

- Content complies with the law and good customs and traditions

## 4.3 Advertising and monetization content

**Violation**

![Violation](https://miniapp.zaloplatforms.com/documents/img/censorship-policy/v2/4.3.a.png)

- The Mini App displays advertising banners without authorization

**Compliant**

![Compliant](https://miniapp.zaloplatforms.com/documents/img/censorship-policy/v2/4.3.b.png)

- The Mini App does not contain or display any monetized advertising in the app

## 4.4 Virtual items

**Violation**

![Violation](https://miniapp.zaloplatforms.com/documents/img/censorship-policy/v2/4.4.a.png)

- The Mini App contains or facilitates the buying and selling of virtual items or virtual currency of electronic games
- The Mini App is involved with cryptocurrency or unlicensed digital products (NFTs)

**Compliant**

![Compliant](https://miniapp.zaloplatforms.com/documents/img/censorship-policy/v2/4.4.b.png)

- The Mini App has no virtual item or cryptocurrency elements

**Violation**

![Violation](https://miniapp.zaloplatforms.com/documents/img/censorship-policy/v2/4.4.c.png)

- The Mini App contains or facilitates the buying and selling of digital content/digital utility services (online courses, music streaming packages, movie streaming packages, etc.)

**Compliant**

![Compliant](https://miniapp.zaloplatforms.com/documents/img/censorship-policy/v2/4.4.d.png)

- The Mini App contains purchase transactions tied to real-world services actually used (laundry, locker rental, charging stations, etc.)

## 4.5 Mini App with cash withdrawal/reward payout features

**Violation**

![Violation](https://miniapp.zaloplatforms.com/documents/img/censorship-policy/v2/4.5.a.png)

- Has features related to cash withdrawal/reward payout

## 4.6 Mini App with social network features or features that directly compete with products in the Zalo system

**Violation**

![Violation](https://miniapp.zaloplatforms.com/documents/img/censorship-policy/v2/4.6.a.png)

- Has social network or user interaction features such as posting posts, videos, likes, comments, etc.
- Has features that directly compete with products in the Zalo system (Zing MP3, Zalo, Video)

## 4.7 Images and text of the app

**Violation**

![Violation](https://miniapp.zaloplatforms.com/documents/img/censorship-policy/v2/4.7.a.png)

- Images fail to load
- Font errors or hard-to-read fonts

**Compliant**

![Compliant](https://miniapp.zaloplatforms.com/documents/img/censorship-policy/v2/4.7.b.png)

- Images and text are displayed clearly

## 4.8 Mini App policy - terms content

**Violation**

![Violation](https://miniapp.zaloplatforms.com/documents/img/censorship-policy/v2/4.8.a.png)

- Generic Policy - Terms content
- Does not clearly indicate which business entity the Policies and Terms belong to

**Compliant**

![Compliant](https://miniapp.zaloplatforms.com/documents/img/censorship-policy/v2/4.8.b.png)

- The title clearly indicates that these are the policy terms of the Mini App "Mini App Name"
- The policy - terms content is clear
