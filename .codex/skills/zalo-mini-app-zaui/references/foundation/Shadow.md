# Shadow

> Source: https://docs.zaloplatforms.com/docs/MA/zaui/foundation/Shadow

Last updated: 28/10/2026

## Shadow: Bottom

Use when you want the shadow below the object; used in most cases.

![Bottom Shadow](https://docs.zaloplatforms.com/assets/MA/img/zaui/shadow/bottom-shadow.png)

## Shadow: Top

Use when you want the shadow above the object; used to create separation for objects that cannot use a bottom shadow (e.g., Bottom Sheet)

![Top Shadow](https://docs.zaloplatforms.com/assets/MA/img/zaui/shadow/top-shadow.png)
